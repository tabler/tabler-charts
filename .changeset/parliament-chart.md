New `parliament` chart: election results as a hemicycle, a dot per seat on concentric benches, each party a wedge of them in data order from the left, with the number of seats in the middle. Options `total` (vacant seats), `rows`, `angle` (`360` for a full ring), `startAngle`, `innerRadius`, `gap`, `format`, `center` and `padding`; entry `@tabler/charts/parliament`, `ParliamentChart` in React and Vue, type `parliament` in the element and the data API.
