`x.rotate: true` turns the category labels below a line, area or vertical bar chart 90°, reading bottom to top, so that a chart with many or long categories keeps a label on every one instead of dropping some. The plot leaves room for the longest label. It does nothing on a time axis or in horizontal bars.
