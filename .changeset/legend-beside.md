Added `legend.position` `'left'`, `'right'` and `'auto'`: the legend sits beside the chart with its entries stacked, and the chart takes the remaining width (sides swap in `rtl`). `'auto'` puts it on the right when the chart keeps two thirds of the container, and below it otherwise. Exports draw it where the page shows it.
