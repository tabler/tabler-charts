New `treemap` chart: parts of a whole as tiles, each as large as its value, squarified with the largest in the top left corner. Entries with `children` are groups whose tiles stay together and share a palette color; names and values are written in the tiles where they fit. Options `gap`, `radius`, `labels`, `format` and `padding`, and `--tc-treemap-opacity` fades the tiles; entry `@tabler/charts/treemap`, `TreemapChart` in React and Vue, type `treemap` in the element and the data API.
